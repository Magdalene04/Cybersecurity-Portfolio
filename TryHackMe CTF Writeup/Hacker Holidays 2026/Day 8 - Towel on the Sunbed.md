# Hacker Holidays 2026 - Towel on the Sunbed
The **Towel on the Sunbed** challenge takes place by the resort's private beach area, where guests attempt to reserve premium spots using automated booking software. 

This room focuses on identifying race conditions and logic flaws in web applications. The objective is to exploit race conditions during resource reservation, manipulate request concurrency to bypass client-side checks, and elevate privileges to secure the flag.
